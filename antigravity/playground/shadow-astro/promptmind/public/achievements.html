Codex of Prompt Engineering

CHAPTER I: The Persona Protocol

By instructing the AI to roleplay as a highly specialized avatar, you restrict its lexical search space, causing it to use professional vocabulary, relevant design paradigms, and avoid generic conversational filler.

Template
Act as a Senior [Expert Title] with 15 years of experience in [Niche Field]. Your background includes [Accomplishments] and you specialize in [Methodologies].

CHAPTER II: Chain of Thought Reasoning

Prompting the model to "think step-by-step" forces the AI to compute linear logical intermediate variables first rather than guessing a response immediately. This raises computation time but yields dramatic logical precision.

Template
Before outputting the final result, outline your logic step-by-step. Dedicate a scratchpad block to evaluate variables, assess trade-offs, and detail intermediate calculations.

CHAPTER III: Few-Shot Calibration

Models adapt quickly to visual cues. Injecting a few inputs and their ideal target outputs ("Few-shot prompting") calibrates tone, format compliance, and output lengths much more effectively than descriptive words.

Template
For context, here are examples of expected outputs: Input: [Example Input 1] Output: [Example Output 1] Input: [Example Input 2] Output: [Example Output 2] Now, process the active prompt: [Active Prompt]

CHAPTER IV: Format & Boundaries Grid

Always define strict boundary conditions. What must the AI *never* do? What schema constraints apply? This prevents model drift and ensures the output matches your software architecture pipeline exactly.

Template
OUTPUT CONSTRAINTS: 1. Provide the response strictly in raw JSON matching the schema: [Schema Detail] 2. DO NOT include code markdown wrappers (```json) or introductory headers. 3. If an error occurs, output {"error": "description"} and cease operations.
